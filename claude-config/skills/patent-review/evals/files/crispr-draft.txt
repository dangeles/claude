UNITED STATES PATENT APPLICATION

TITLE: Base Editing Compositions for Correction of Sickle Cell Disease Mutations

BACKGROUND
Sickle cell disease (SCD) is caused by a single nucleotide mutation in the HBB gene encoding beta-globin, specifically an A-to-T transversion at codon 6 (E6V). Traditional CRISPR-Cas9 approaches generate double-strand breaks (DSBs) to correct this mutation, but DSBs risk undesired indels and chromosomal rearrangements. Base editors, which couple a catalytically impaired Cas9 (nickase or dead Cas9) with a deaminase domain, enable precise single-nucleotide changes without DSBs. Adenine base editors (ABEs) catalyze A-to-G transitions. Since the SCD mutation is an A-to-T transversion, correction to the wild-type adenine at codon 6 requires a T-to-A reversion, which is not achievable with current ABEs.

SUMMARY
The present invention provides novel base editor compositions and guide RNAs for correction of the sickle cell disease mutation in hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells (HSPCs). The base editors comprise a cytosine base editor (CBE) paired with a guide RNA that positions the target cytosine on the non-template strand within the editing window.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION
The SCD mutation (HBB c.17A>T; p.Glu6Val) can be corrected to wild-type sequence using a cytosine base editor targeting the complementary strand, where the mutant thymine appears as a cytosine. The CBE of the present invention comprises a nCas9 (D10A nickase) fused to an evolved rAPOBEC1 deaminase with reduced bystander editing, connected by a 16-amino acid XTEN linker.

The guide RNA is designed with a 20-nucleotide spacer sequence that positions the target C (corresponding to the mutant T on the coding strand) at position 4 of the editing window (positions 4-8 counting from the PAM-distal end of the protospacer).

The PAM sequence used is NGG (SpCas9 PAM). A suitable guide RNA spacer sequence is: 5'-GTAACGGCAGACTTCTCCTC-3'.

Editing efficiency in CD34+ HSPCs from SCD donors reaches 40-65% of alleles following electroporation of ribonucleoprotein (RNP) complexes.

CLAIMS

1. A base editor composition comprising:
(a) a Cas9 nickase (nCas9) having the D10A mutation; and
(b) a cytosine deaminase domain.

2. The base editor composition of claim 1, wherein the cytosine deaminase domain is an evolved rAPOBEC1 deaminase.

3. The base editor composition of claim 1, further comprising a guide RNA comprising a spacer sequence complementary to a region of the HBB gene containing the sickle cell mutation.

4. A method of correcting the sickle cell disease mutation in a cell, comprising introducing into the cell the base editor composition of claim 1 and a guide RNA targeting the HBB sickle cell mutation.

5. The method of claim 4, wherein the cell is a hematopoietic stem cell.

ABSTRACT
Base editor compositions comprising a Cas9 nickase fused to a cytosine deaminase are provided for precise correction of the sickle cell disease mutation in HBB. Methods of correcting the SCD mutation in hematopoietic stem cells are also provided.
