UNITED STATES PATENT APPLICATION

TITLE: Selective KRAS G12C Inhibitors and Methods of Use

BACKGROUND
KRAS is the most frequently mutated oncogene in human cancers, with mutations found in approximately 25% of all tumors. The G12C mutation (glycine to cysteine substitution at codon 12) accounts for roughly 13% of KRAS mutations and is particularly prevalent in non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The G12C mutation locks KRAS in a constitutively active GTP-bound state by impairing GTPase activity. Small molecule inhibitors that covalently bind the mutant cysteine residue in the GDP-bound (inactive) state of KRAS G12C have emerged as a promising therapeutic approach.

SUMMARY
The present invention provides novel compounds of Formula (I) that selectively and irreversibly inhibit KRAS G12C by forming a covalent bond with Cys12 in the switch II pocket (S-IIP). The compounds exhibit IC50 values of less than 10 nM against KRAS G12C in biochemical assays, and demonstrate greater than 100-fold selectivity against wild-type KRAS and other RAS family members.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Formula (I) compounds comprise an acrylamide warhead for irreversible covalent engagement with Cys12, connected via a linker to a core scaffold that occupies the S-IIP. Preferred compounds include those wherein the core scaffold is a tetrahydroisoquinoline (THIQ) moiety substituted at the 6-position with a fluorine atom and at the 2-position with a sulfonyl group.

In vitro studies demonstrate that exemplary compound 7a inhibits KRAS G12C with an IC50 of 3.2 nM and shows >200-fold selectivity versus wild-type KRAS (IC50 = 685 nM). In cellular assays using the NCI-H358 NSCLC cell line (homozygous KRAS G12C), compound 7a inhibits pERK levels with an IC50 of 18 nM.

The compounds are administered orally. Suitable doses range from 100 mg to 600 mg once daily.

ABSTRACT
Novel compounds of Formula (I) that selectively inhibit KRAS G12C through irreversible covalent bond formation are described, along with pharmaceutical compositions and methods of treating KRAS G12C-mutant cancers.
